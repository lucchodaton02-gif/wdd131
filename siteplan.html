Website Planning Document

Site Name

Benin Explorer

I chose the name Benin Explorer because the website is about discovering interesting places and attractions in Benin. The name is simple and easy to remember.

Site Purpose

The purpose of Benin Explorer is to help people discover places to visit in Benin. The website will provide information about tourist attractions, cultural places, activities, and useful information for visitors.

Scenarios

Visitors to the website may ask questions such as:

  • What are the best places to visit in Benin?
  • What places can I visit in Cotonou?
  • What cultural and historical places can I discover?
  • What activities can I do during my visit to Benin?

Color Schema

Green - #087F5B

Green will be used for headings, navigation, and important website elements.

Gold - #F2C94C

Gold will be used for accents, buttons, and highlights.

White - #FFFFFF

White will be used mainly for the background and content areas.

Typography

Poppins

Poppins will be used for headings, the logo, and navigation.

Arial

Arial will be used for paragraphs and general content because it is simple and easy to read.
