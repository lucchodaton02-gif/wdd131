Benin

Benin is a country located in West Africa. Its capital city is Porto-Novo, while Cotonou is the largest city and an important economic center. Benin is known for its rich culture, history, traditions, and beautiful landscapes.

The country has a diverse population and many different languages and cultural traditions. Benin is also historically connected to the ancient Kingdom of Dahomey.
